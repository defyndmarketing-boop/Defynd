Website Design + Hosting vs DIY: What's Actually Cheaper?

DIY builders promise a website for "R99/month". Many South African owners pay for a year, then discover the real cost: hosting upsells, SSL, plugins, premium domains . and 20 hours of their own time. Here's the honest maths.

The DIY trap in South Africa

A free builder gets you online, but not ranked. You pay to remove ads, connect your domain, add SSL, and buy storage. That's R200-R600/month before you write a single page that ranks for "web design [your city]". Add your time at R300/hour and DIY costs more than done-for-you in month two.

What a done-for-you package includes

  • Custom design that matches your brand (no templates)
  • Hosting, SSL, and domain included in the development package
  • Copywriting and image sourcing
  • Google Business basics and SEO structure
  • WhatsApp CTAs so enquiries find you instantly
Rule of thumb: if you'll spend more than 3 weekends building, hire it out. Your time sells your service . not drags templates.

Which should you pick?

Choose DIY only if you enjoy tech and have time. Choose done-for-you if you need trust, speed, and Google rankings. Most SMEs in South Africa earn back their package from one client who found them on Google.
