The SME Website Checklist for South Africa (2026)

Your website is your receptionist 24/7. If it fails any of these 12, visitors leave . and Google stops ranking you.

The 12 non-negotiables

  • Mobile-perfect: 70%+ of SA traffic is mobile. Test on a cheap Android, not just your iPhone.
  • Loads in under 3 seconds: Compressed images, no bloated builders. Ours are static and fast.
  • WhatsApp on every page: Floating button + prefilled message ("Hi, I want a quote").
  • Own-domain email: you@yourbusiness.co.za beats Gmail every time for trust.
  • SSL + hosting you own: Padlock in the bar, domain in your name.
  • Clear value in headline: Who you help + what you do in 8 seconds.
  • Google Business linked: Same NAP (name, address, phone) everywhere.
  • SEO titles: "Web design [city] | Your Brand" . not "Home".
  • Load-shedding proof: Hosted on always-on infrastructure, not a home server.
  • Trust proof: Process, FAQs, ownership promise . even before testimonials exist.
  • Working contact: Form that actually emails you (ours goes to sales@).
  • Analytics: Google Search Console + GA4 from day one.
Fail 3 or more? Your site is costing you enquiries. Fix the top 3 this month.
